\section*{1. Archive selection and provenance standards}
Public archives were selected for inclusion when they provided (i) spatially resolved molecular measurements with recoverable physical scale, (ii) identifiable biological groups or an explicitly scoped tissue comparison, and (iii) a directly retrievable official accession. Studies with unresolved donor independence, unverified biopsy-to-donor crosswalks, animal models, or longitudinal paired designs are retained in a separate supplementary catalog with explicit caveats rather than merged into the primary independent-donor benchmark.

Original biological conclusions and statistical procedures are strictly scoped to the specific modality, figure, or Methods passage documented in each source article. No biological sample size is ever inferred from file counts alone. Public audit results and synthetic software controls reside in separate directories, each audit report JSON declares its provenance kind and source URL (auxiliary benchmark records such as memory profiles record their provenance kind separately), and the interactive observatory keeps any uncomputed archive record in an explicit unassessed state.

\section*{2. Finite-volume discrete diffusion}
Let $h$ denote the square control-volume width (in $\mu\mathrm{m}$) and $A$ the four-neighbor adjacency matrix over occupied control volumes. The cell-centered graph Laplacian is defined as $L = (D - A)/h^2$, where $D$ is the diagonal degree matrix. Under this construction, any constant field carries zero flux across tissue boundaries, and removing an empty control volume automatically eliminates its incident interfaces. Observed spots, cells, or pixels are averaged within each occupied control volume.

Several discretization properties govern physical-scale interpretation:
\begin{itemize}
\item Sub-volume variations are averaged by the grid, and shifting the grid origin or rotating a tissue section need not preserve the discrete four-neighbor graph identically.
\item Binning a staggered hexagonal capture array (such as classic Visium) onto square control volumes can merge nearby spots with unequal occupancy.
\item Near the discrete grid cutoff, spectral response becomes anisotropic; nominal wavelengths therefore require explicit checks of spatial footprint support and grid dispersion.
\end{itemize}
A shared spectral interval bounds the Laplacians of all sections in a cohort. Bandpass filtering is implemented as a difference of heat kernels evaluated via a common Chebyshev polynomial expansion, whose truncation order controls numerical approximation accuracy across sections.

\section*{3. Within-band orthogonal projection and white-noise gain}
Let $H$ denote the fine-band filter operator and $Q$ an orthonormal numerical basis for the column space of the filtered covariate matrix $HC$. The projected residual operator is $B = (I - QQ^\top)H$. For independent unit-variance white noise across $n$ occupied volumes belonging to connected components that support the fine wavelength, the expected filtered noise energy per volume is $\mathrm{tr}(B^\top B)/n$. When covariate rank is large relative to $n$, preliminary projection onto unfiltered covariates can slightly attenuate the expected second-difference noise estimate; the reported intercept applies no leverage adjustment for that preliminary step. The implementation computes the trace directly on small grids and uses fixed-seed stochastic trace probes on larger grids.

Along any matched straight three-volume path with values $(x_a, x_m, x_b)$, the two-lag semivariogram intercept reduces to the path average of $(x_a - 2x_m + x_b)^2 / 6$. This quantity is strictly nonnegative and equals the white-noise variance in expectation for a locally linear field perturbed by homoscedastic independent noise. To prevent truncation bias under unequal precision across donors, patient-level tests retain the signed noise-adjusted residual energy, including negative values when estimated noise exceeds observed band energy. Strong tissue discontinuities, spatial anisotropy, spatially correlated instrument noise, heterogeneous control-volume occupancy, and TIC self-inclusion can all perturb this approximation. On log-normalized inputs, the diagnostic characterizes noise sensitivity on the transformed scale rather than diagnosing a negative-binomial count mechanism.

Every cohort JSON records per-section projection ranks, effective band dimension, grid widths, edge counts, and trace-probe settings. A connected two-dimensional tissue footprint must span the target wavelength, and the wavelength must be at least $\pi h$. The engine estimates the spectral participation ratio $(\mathrm{tr}\,H^2)^2 / \mathrm{tr}\,H^4$ on that support and excludes a section fit whenever both the ratio of projection rank to effective dimension and the projected white-noise energy fraction meet the prespecified saturation threshold.

\section*{4. Screening thresholds, simulation endpoints, and scope}
Traffic-light cutoffs (green, yellow, red) are descriptive screening conventions rather than universal biological or Type~I error boundaries. Exact permutation floors follow the inclusive doubled-tail convention, and missing metadata is always distinguished from a tested null result. When Monte Carlo sampling is required, label allocations are regenerated deterministically from a fixed seed and the observed group sizes.

In the synthetic phase diagram, the raw endpoint denotes total filtered fine-band energy prior to covariate projection, whereas the adjusted endpoint denotes residual energy after both covariate projection and nugget subtraction. By contrast, in the public cohort nugget reports, raw denotes fine-band energy after covariate projection and prior to nugget subtraction. Each simulation cell evaluates an absolute mean-contrast permutation tail for a single feature across replicates sharing one allocation set, so reported Wilson confidence intervals condition on that reference allocation set. Under the simulation null, latent biological amplitude is matched in distribution across arms while mean abundance, composition, or batch alignment may still differ between groups. Consequently, minimum-donor lookup values are specific to the evaluated generative family and uncertainty rule.

\section*{5. Deterministic software fixtures and memory benchmark}
Four built-in synthetic fixtures exercise predeclared audit paths: (i) a balanced mirror null that reuses random spatial fields across arms as a deterministic negative control, (ii) a patient-offset pseudoreplication trap, (iii) a measured composition and mean-linked noise trap, and (iv) complete acquisition-stage separation. These fixtures verify deterministic software behavior; statistical error rates over random cohorts are estimated separately by the replicated phase diagram.

The scalability benchmark profiles a fresh Python process from backed HDF5 input ingestion through all five audit dimensions and strict JSON serialization. The reported \RSSGiB{}~GiB peak resident memory applies specifically to the documented \MemoryFeatures{}-feature sparse workload and host environment; synthetic input generation occurs outside the timed process.

\section*{6. End-to-end reproduction and verification}
To reproduce the full release pipeline from a clean checkout:
\begin{enumerate}
\item Install the package with development, agent, archive, and browser extras using \texttt{pip install -e ".[dev,agent,archives,browser]"}, and install Chromium for offline dashboard checks via \texttt{python -m playwright install chromium}.
\item Execute \texttt{spatial-audit demo} to verify the four synthetic controls, then run \texttt{spatial-audit run} on any prepared cohort manifest.
\item Run the archive retrieval scripts, which log exact byte sizes and SHA-256 digests alongside upstream checksums when available.
\item With \texttt{pdflatex} and \texttt{bibtex} on \texttt{PATH}, run the verification scripts in \texttt{scripts/}: \texttt{verify\_\allowbreak dois.py} checks bibliographic metadata, \texttt{run\_\allowbreak benchmark\_\allowbreak suite.py} rebuilds the offline dashboard bundles, and \texttt{verify\_\allowbreak release\_\allowbreak gate.py} validates all strict JSON artifacts, portable paths, unit and browser tests, static analysis, LaTeX layout, and wheel/sdist installation.
\end{enumerate}
Browser-uploaded JSON reports are parsed strictly in client memory without network transmission, and the local MCP stdio server exposes the same deterministic audit records produced by the command-line interface.

\section*{7. Validation experiments and candidate archives}
The raw and Anscombe benchmarks enumerate every balanced allocation at four, five and seven donors per arm. At each size, baseline multipliers of 1, 1.5 and 2 cross negative-binomial dispersions of 0, 0.1 and 0.35. The simulation uses one observation per lattice site and the same production filter, covariate projection and signed nugget subtraction in both modes. The transformation $2\sqrt{x+3/8}$ applies to the occupied-volume mean before filtering. A raw-scale amplitude increase of seven units defines the alternative.

In GSE348499, each split assigns the smaller pseudo-arm uniformly without replacement and keeps every cell of each donor together. Pooled Mann--Whitney probabilities use midranks, tie correction and continuity correction. For each endpoint, donor tests use 2,047 random allocations with the plus-one tail rule. The BH family comprises eligible features for that endpoint within a single split. The empirical-null JSON retains donor assignments, seeds, denominators and per-split call counts. At 32 and 2,512 features the cortex comparison checks that section input digests and the ordered feature intersection agree. The lung fibrosis check compares donor-level and native R calls on the same ordered panel.

The four scaling runs reuse the same spatial extent, requested sparsity and seed. Fixture generation precedes timing; the audit timer starts immediately before manifest ingestion and stops after report serialization. Peak RSS uses the worker's process high-water mark. Paired inference requires both conditions for each donor. Stratified tests condition on within-stratum arm counts. The optional Voronoi construction clips cells to the convex hull and weights sites equally when summarizing energy. For the MSI sensitivity run each target window is removed from its own TIC before filtering the covariate; raster coordinates remain in the projection.

The targeted CosMx search examined official GEO metadata for GSE348495, GSE312757 and GSE311636. GSE348495 supplies tissue and sample labels in the downloaded cell table, including repeated tumour prefixes. A biopsy-to-donor and adjacent-tissue crosswalk remained unresolved. GSE312757 describes within-specimen regions from liver metastases; GSE311636 supplies RNA and protein assay identifiers. The inspected files did not establish independent two-group donor contrasts for these candidates. The screening record preserves the inspected sample titles, URLs and SHA-256 digests.
